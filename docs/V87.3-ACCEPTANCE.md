## Delivered

- After new-passport recovery-code confirmation, the first map view automatically opens Ari's floating welcome dialog. This replaces the inline information block. An actual native modal supplies a dimmed backdrop, background scroll lock, focus containment, Escape-to-defer, visible keyboard focus and in-dialog language controls. Native form navigation and a visible overlay remain usable without scripting.
- Five account-persisted chapters introduce the island overview, square, cottage, woodland and beach. Seven places are shown in an explicitly schematic activity map. The overview also explains the living street, sports activities and Nomad House with actual entry links. Each chapter says what to do and what it saves/unlocks. No shell gathering, economy or farming is advertised as currently playable.
- Previous/next, defer/resume from the saved chapter, optional early scene entry, and replay are supported. Existing accounts are not forced into onboarding. A final destination choice completes the guide and redirects straight to an allowlisted actual scene; it cannot redirect externally. Tour completion never fabricates exploration events or rewards.
- Custom greeting editor at the harbor and square includes retained draft text from the last saved greeting, a live plain-text preview and character count, current-public-text readback, publication and withdrawal. It uses the existing same-origin authenticated server action, escaping, bounded filtering and persistent cooldown. Harbor publication returns to the harbor with a saved status. Greetings are shared on the existing square noticeboard, not on a new duplicated social feed.
- Adventure journal cards identify main/side/daily/hidden quests and show individual completed actions and links to missing actions, alongside reward and claim state. Collection cards remain non-currency keepsakes. NPC invitations show the existing resident portrait and explain accept → cottage conversation → journal claim.
- New content uses the existing Traditional Chinese/English catalog. No new identity, permission, furniture, currency or collection subsystem.
