## Playable areas

- Work Lounge: two selectable player chairs, bamboo canopy, timber table, rattan seating, laptops, book and plants. Seat then work/read/rest. Existing fullBody portraits and limb styles show selected species/clothing with typing/reading/rest poses.
- Coffee Corner: modeled counter, machine, cups and dessert case, plus a selectable rattan chair. Reuses the exact existing `townLifeClient` + `drinkArt` script/protocol and all three original virtual drinks. The one player avatar carries the shared cup; steam/sip feedback adds no duplicate player or server drink system.
- Slow Deck: selectable lounger, rest/stretch/daydream modes, tropical greenery and lightly animated water. Standing and stroll actions restore movement after leaving a seat.
- Focus Nook: individual work/read seat and optional Reduce motion and sound setting. Quiet preference survives refresh locally. Sound is OFF by default and uses a small generated/filtered breeze only after explicit activation; toggle/quiet/pagehide suspend it. This is synthesized ambience, not a recorded soundscape.

The interior uses the existing projected SVG scene approach and fullBody avatar system, with static dimensional furniture faces/contact shading, animated sprite poses and a fixed walk graph. It is not a new engine or a physically simulated/rigged 3D interior. On narrow screens the scene scrolls horizontally with 44px controls and area shortcuts rather than shrinking touch targets. A seating action disables conflicting choices until arrival; seat/mode/leave transitions reject duplicate or invalid events. Player activities/drinks are session-local; no new game records are written.
